Interpretation
--------------
The model's two largest confusion pairs both involve the "Shirt" class being
mistaken for "T-shirt/top" or "Coat", and vice versa. This is plausible: at
28x28 grayscale resolution, shirts, t-shirts, and coats are all upper-body
garments with broadly similar silhouettes (rectangular torso, two sleeves),
and the low resolution removes fine-grained cues (collar shape, sleeve
length, fabric texture) that would normally distinguish them. This is
reflected in "Shirt" having the lowest precision (0.750) and recall (0.696)
of all 10 classes -- it is the category the model finds hardest to separate
from its visually similar neighbours.
